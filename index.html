Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: How is reading time calculated?

A: Reading time is calculated based on an average adult reading speed of 200 words per minute (WPM).

Q: Is my text, image, or SVG code saved on any server?

A: No. All calculations, SVG renders, and image conversions happen entirely inside your browser using JavaScript and Canvas APIs. No data ever leaves your device.

Q: Why should I convert images to WebP or use SVGs?

A: WebP images are typically 25% to 35% smaller than JPG or PNG files without losing quality. SVGs provide resolution-independent vector graphics that load fast and look sharp on all displays.
