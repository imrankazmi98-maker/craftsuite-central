Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: How is reading time calculated?

A: Reading time is calculated based on an average adult reading speed of 200 words per minute (WPM).

Q: Is my text or image saved on any server?

A: No. All calculations and image conversions happen entirely inside your browser using JavaScript and Canvas APIs. No data ever leaves your device.

Q: Why should I convert images to WebP?

A: WebP images are typically 25% to 35% smaller than JPG or PNG files without losing perceptible quality, directly improving website loading performance and SEO rankings.
