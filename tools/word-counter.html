How to use the Word & Character Counter

  1. Paste or type your text into the box above.
  2. Watch the word, character, sentence and line counts update live.
  3. Use the reading time estimate to gauge how long the text takes to read.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. The counting happens with JavaScript directly in your browser tab - nothing is sent to a server.

How is reading time calculated?

It is estimated at 200 words per minute, a common average adult reading speed.

Does it count spaces as characters?

The main character count includes spaces; a separate stat shows the count without spaces.
