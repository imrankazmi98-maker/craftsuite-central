Remove Duplicate Lines

Paste a list to instantly remove duplicate lines and sort entries alphabetically.

How to use the Remove Duplicate Lines

  1. Paste your list, one entry per line.
  2. Click Remove Duplicates to keep only unique lines.
  3. Optionally click Sort Alphabetically, then copy the result.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the comparison case-sensitive?

Yes, 'Apple' and 'apple' are treated as different lines.

Does it remove empty lines?

Empty lines are treated as entries too; remove them manually before running the tool if needed.
