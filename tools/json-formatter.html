JSON Formatter & Validator

Beautify, minify and validate JSON instantly in your browser.

How to use the JSON Formatter & Validator

  1. Paste your raw JSON into the left box.
  2. Click Beautify to pretty-print it, or Minify to compress it.
  3. Copy the result from the right box.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if my JSON is invalid?

The tool shows an error message describing what is wrong with the syntax.

Is there a size limit?

It runs in your browser's memory, so very large files may be slow depending on your device.
