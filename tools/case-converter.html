Text Case Converter

Convert text to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, url-slug-format or camelCase instantly.

How to use the Text Case Converter

  1. Type or paste your text into the box.
  2. Click the case style you need - the text updates in place.
  3. Press Copy Text to copy the converted result.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is url-slug-format used for?

It turns a title into a clean, hyphenated URL slug, e.g. 'My Blog Post' becomes 'my-blog-post'.

Can I convert code variable names?

Yes, use camelCase for JavaScript-style variable naming.
