Length Converter

Convert between metres, kilometres, centimetres, miles, yards, feet and inches instantly.

-

Why use this tool?

Length conversion is a daily need when reading recipes, furniture dimensions or travel distances given in a unit system you are not used to, or when working across metric and imperial measurements in construction and engineering.

How to use the Length Converter

  1. Enter the value you want to convert.
  2. Choose the unit you're converting from and to.
  3. Click Convert to see the result.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this precise enough for engineering work?

Conversions use standard exact factors, but always double-check critical measurements with a second source for safety-critical work.

Can I convert nautical miles?

Not currently; this tool covers the most common metric and imperial land units.
