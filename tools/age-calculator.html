Why use this tool?

Knowing an exact age down to the month and day is useful for legal forms, visa applications, eligibility checks (age limits for jobs or competitions) or simply settling a birthday debate.

How to use the Age Calculator

  1. Pick your date of birth.
  2. Optionally change the 'calculate on' date (defaults to today).
  3. Click Calculate Age to see the exact breakdown.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it account for leap years?

Yes, the calculation uses actual calendar dates so leap years are handled correctly.

Can I calculate age as of a past or future date?

Yes, change the second date field to any date you need.
