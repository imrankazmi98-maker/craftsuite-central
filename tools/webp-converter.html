How to use the Image to WebP Converter

  1. Choose a PNG or JPG image from your device.
  2. Adjust the quality slider if you want a smaller file size.
  3. Click Convert Now, preview the result, then download the WebP file.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. Conversion happens locally using the browser's canvas API.

What quality setting should I use?

0.8 is a good balance between file size and visual quality for most photos.
