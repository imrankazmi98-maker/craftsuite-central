Discount Calculator

Work out the final price and the amount you save on a discounted item.

-Final Price
-You Save

Why use this tool?

Shoppers use this to quickly check whether a 'sale price' is actually a good deal, and sellers use it to price promotions consistently across a store or menu.

How to use the Discount Calculator

  1. Enter the original price.
  2. Enter the discount percentage being offered.
  3. Click Calculate to see the final price and total savings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I stack multiple discounts?

Calculate the first discount, then run the result through the calculator again with the second discount percentage.

Does this include tax?

No, this calculates the discount only; add tax separately if needed.
