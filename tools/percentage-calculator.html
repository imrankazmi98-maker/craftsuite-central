Why use this tool?

Percentages come up everywhere: calculating a tip, working out a grade, comparing sales growth between quarters, or figuring out how much of a budget has been spent. This tool covers the three most common percentage questions in one place.

How to use the Percentage Calculator

  1. Pick the calculation type that matches your question.
  2. Enter your numbers into the fields.
  3. Click Calculate next to that row to see the result.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is percentage change calculated?

As ((new value - old value) / old value) x 100; a negative result means a decrease.

Can I use decimal numbers?

Yes, all three calculators accept decimals.
