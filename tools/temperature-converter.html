Temperature Converter

Convert between Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin instantly.

Why use this tool?

Weather reports, cooking temperatures and scientific readings often use different scales depending on the country or context - this converter avoids the mental math of switching between them.

How to use the Temperature Converter

  1. Enter the temperature value.
  2. Choose the scale you're converting from and to.
  3. Click Convert.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 0°C in Fahrenheit?

0°C equals 32°F, the freezing point of water.

Why is Kelvin used in science?

Kelvin starts at absolute zero, making it convenient for scientific formulas that would otherwise need to handle negative temperatures.
